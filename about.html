About Cohere

Welcome to Cohere, the ultimate platform for discovering concerts and collecting memories. Whether you're here to find your next unforgettable live music experience or to curate your personal Music Passport, you're in the right place.

Instructions

  • Discover: Browse through upcoming concerts and find live events based on your favorite artists, genres, and location.
  • Live Rooms: Join interactive sessions and connect with other fans in real-time before, during, and after a show.
  • Music Passport: Claim your digital stamps for concerts you've attended. Build your personal history of live music experiences and showcase your unique fan journey.
  • Get Started: Click on the "Get Started" button in the menu or go to the Login page to create your account and begin your Cohere adventure.

Ready to start? Head over to the Login Page to join the community.
